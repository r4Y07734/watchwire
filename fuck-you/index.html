Fuck You

10 October 2026

Pete Hegseth

U.S. Secretary of Defense

Why

For deciding to livestream and publicly broadcast the execution of the Fort Hood shooter by firing squad, turning state-sanctioned capital punishment into a digital spectacle.

The full story

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth announced that the upcoming execution of Nidal Hasan—the former Army major convicted of the 2009 mass shooting at Fort Hood—will be conducted publicly via a livestreamed firing squad in Texas. This marks a radical departure from modern American penal practice, representing what critics describe as the first public execution in the U.S. in nearly a century.

The decision has drawn sharp condemnation from lawmakers, human rights advocates, and military experts who argue that turning an execution into a public spectacle degrades institutional dignity, promotes a culture of vengeance, and provides adversaries with immediate propaganda. Rather than upholding justice with solemnity, the move transforms a somber penal event into content for social media and public consumption.
